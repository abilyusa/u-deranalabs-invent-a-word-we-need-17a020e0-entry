## The entry

> Cancelight (n.): the secret relief you feel when plans are cancelled.

One newly coined English word — *cancel* + *delight* — with a single clear
definition line. It names a feeling English has no word for: the quiet,
slightly guilty relief when a plan falls through and your evening is suddenly
yours again. Sayable, spellable, memorable, and ready to use in a sentence:
"Total cancelight tonight — dinner got cancelled."
